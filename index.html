Philosophy

How I think about design

01

The space between things

This is where things break. A product deadline vs. a technical limitation, a design vision vs. the system that makes it scale, what exists today, and needs to exist tomorrow. Reducing these frictions is how good work actually ships.

02

Prototypes over decks

A well-placed prototype convinces faster than a deck ever will. I'd rather sketch a flow, build a quick interactive mock, or walk through real data than argue a point in the abstract — proof beats persuasion.

03

Zooming in and out

From pixels and tokens to roadmap, I move across every level without losing the thread. A button's padding and a quarter's strategic priorities. I solve puzzles at the product and system level.

04

Process shapes culture

Not the other way around. The systems we put in place, the way teams hand off work, the quality of what we document: that's what determines how a team actually operates.
