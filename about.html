Oh, hello

I'm Mathieu — Senior Product Designer living in Amsterdam since 2015

I love solving puzzles at both product and system level, bringing clarity out of complexity through elegant, simple solutions that make sense to people. My process blends collaboration, strategy, and constant learning.

Right now, I am working at Eneco , an international energy company present in 4 countries. My primary focus is on Eneco's design system, Spark.

In my spare time, you’ll often find me vibe-coding, woodworking, or bikepacking.
